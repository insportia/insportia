Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Insportia ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you use insportia.org, our Telegram channels and paid plans (the "Service"). We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the Service.

1. Data we collect

  • Data you give us. When you contact us or subscribe, we may receive your name, Telegram username, email address and the content of your messages.
  • Payment data. Payments are processed by independent payment providers. We never see or store your full card details; we receive only confirmation of payment, the plan purchased and a transaction reference.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, used for security and basic analytics.
  • Cookies. We use only strictly necessary cookies and, where enabled, basic analytics cookies to understand site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How we use data

  • To provide the Service, manage subscriptions and grant channel access;
  • To answer your questions and provide support;
  • To run the monthly giveaway and contact winners;
  • To secure the Service and prevent abuse;
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, lawful requests).

Legal bases under the GDPR, where it applies: performance of a contract (subscriptions, support), legitimate interests (security, basic analytics) and consent (optional communications), which you may withdraw at any time.

3. What we never do

We do not sell or rent your personal data, we do not use it for third-party advertising, and we do not send marketing messages without your consent.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers who help us operate — hosting, payment processing, and the Telegram platform itself — each bound by their own privacy obligations, and with authorities where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above: support messages up to 24 months, subscription and transaction records as required by tax and accounting law, and technical logs for a short security-review period.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (including under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, contact us via Telegram or support@insportia.org — we respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party platforms

Telegram and any payment provider process your data under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read. Sports fixture data displayed on the site is retrieved from public third-party sources and involves no personal data of yours.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time; the date above shows the current version. Material changes will be announced on our Telegram channel.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: t.me/insportia · support@insportia.org
