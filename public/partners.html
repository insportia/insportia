Become a Partner

Content creators, channel owners, media buyers and brands — let's grow together.

Insportia works with partners who reach sports audiences and care about quality. If you run a Telegram channel, a website, a YouTube or TikTok account, or you buy media professionally, we would love to hear from you.

Who we partner with

  • Content creators & tipsters — cross-promotion and revenue-sharing collaborations with sports channels and pages;
  • Website owners — placements and co-created content on sports and analytics sites;
  • Media buyers — performance-based campaigns for our membership plans;
  • Brands & services — sponsorships and placements inside our channel, where the offer genuinely fits our audience.
  • iGaming brands with offers — we primarily work on hybrid deals: CPA + RevShare is the model that interests us most. Flat placements are possible, but hybrid gets priority.

What we offer

  • Transparent, agreed-in-advance terms — flat fee, revenue share or hybrid, depending on the format;
  • Fast communication: one contact person, replies typically within hours;
  • On-time payouts, every time — our reputation depends on it;
  • Creative support: our team can prepare promo materials adapted to your audience and language.

How to apply

No forms, no waiting. Just message us on Telegram — tell us who you are, what audience or traffic you have and what kind of partnership you have in mind. We reply to every serious message, typically within a few hours.

Ready to talk? Our team is one tap away.
